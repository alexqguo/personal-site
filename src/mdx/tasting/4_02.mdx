It is with a Barberton daisy that I welcome you all to our first **wine** tasting since 2018!! Today we will be sampling a special import all the way from Nagano prefecture's very own Daiou Wasabi Farm!! 長野県安曇野市大王わさび農場からの商品です！ More specifically, we are having a **wasabi white wine** (12.5% ABV). 

The Daiou Wasabi Farm is one of Japan's largest wasabi farming sites, with multiple large fields and streams for cultivation, and even a number of tourist attractions like boutique shops and restaurants as well as a walking tour! The wasabi, a notoriously difficult plant to grow, is nourished with fresh flowing water directly from the Northern Alps. Plants are further covered by black tarp during the hottest months of the year to keep temperatures down and the wasabi happy. 

Look deeper within the farm's various walking paths and you may come across a small cave and shrine dedicated to Hachimen Daiou, an ancient local hero, the farm's namesake and protector. Once you have paid your respects, and perhaps caught a glimpse of the filming site of Akira Kurosawa's 1989 film "Dreams", you can grab a wasabi soft serve ice cream, curry, or other local goods flavored with the farm's very best. I chose to go with the white wine. 

While Daiou may have contributed the wasabi side of the equation, the wine was actually produced by Izutsu (株式会社井筒ワイン) located in nearby Shiojiri city (塩尻市). Your fun fact of the day is that Shiojiri translates to English literally as "salty ass"! Salty or not, the grapes in this wine are actually produced on site in Shiojiri. A true local harmony of fresh ingredients!

The bottle instructs that the wine can be enjoyed before, during or after a meal, and is on the sweeter side. I invite you to enjoy this unique experience with us.
